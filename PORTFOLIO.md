## LinkedIn post draft

I just built Kameti AI with my friend for the PayPal AI Hackathon.

A kameti is a rotating savings circle — something a lot of families in Pakistan and across immigrant communities already do on WhatsApp and trust. Ten people put in $100 a month, one person takes the $1,000 pot each month.

The problem is, it breaks when someone stops paying. So we built an app that fixes that.

Kameti AI tracks who paid, scores every member's reliability from their payment history, explains that score in plain English with AI, and pays the pot out through PayPal.

What I worked on: the PayPal Sandbox integration (Checkout + Payouts), the trust score, and the dashboard. My friend built the frontend and the member flow.

It runs on a fully free stack — PayPal Sandbox, Gemini free tier, Vercel — and no real money moves in the demo.

We are still testing it with a small group, and I would love feedback — especially from anyone who has been in a kameti or committee before.
